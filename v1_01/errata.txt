Issues with the v1.01 PCB
-------------------------

The Tang Nano Footprint holes are a bit small; increase to 0.9mm

The PS/2 Footprint holes are a bit small; increase to 0.9mm. The
connectors Alan used (the green/purple PS/2 sockets from AliExpress I
think) do fit OK, but the connectors I sourced from CPC don't, unless
you carefully file down the pins with a needle file.

The Phono footprint has six unnecessary mounting holes. The best way
of fitting is to trim the centre pin of the socket so the 6 plastic
legs will go down into the holes.

U6 symbol/footprint (TO92) incorrect.

RTC trimmer capactor footprint pads are spaced to far apart. The part
can just about be soldered between them, but it's quite tricky. Move
the pads closer together. It would be nice if this could also
accomodate a fixed 1206 capacitor.

The four joystick channels are connected backwards into the ADS1115
ADC module. This can easily be fixed in the FPGA, so I don't propose
to fix it future PCBs, as that would create compatibility issues.
